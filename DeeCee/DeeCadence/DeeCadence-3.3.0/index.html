What's new · November 2026

A calmer focus, right on time.

A quick look at what changed in this update. It only appears once.

A nudge at the right moment

DeeCadence now tells you when a focus block ends, and reminds you before tasks with a time in your plan. Permission is asked after your first block, never on launch. Set the lead time in Settings → Notifications.

Keep DeeCadence close

Add it to your home screen for quicker access. On Android and desktop, look for the install banner; on iPhone, tap Share → Add to Home Screen. It is also in Settings.

A calmer interface, by default

Motion, glow and shimmer now start off for a quieter focus. If you miss the pulsing orbs, switch them back on in Settings. The first screen and Today's rank ring were tidied up on small screens too.

Find your own focus rhythm

Choose Adaptive in session setup and DeeCadence shapes unequal blocks around your history — an easier start, then longer focus with recovery between. Tell us whether it feels better.
