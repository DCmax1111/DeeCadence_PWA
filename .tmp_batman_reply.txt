Batman-Style Playbook (compact)
- Core rules: lawful, mission-first, non-coercive, protect human dignity.
- Intelligence: continuous OSINT + vetted human experts; daily risk dashboard (policy, finance, supply chains).
- Structure: layered vehicles (foundations, LLCs, impact fund) for legal separation and operational flexibility.
- Teams: small, specialised cells — legal/compliance, ops, impact investing, field ops, comms, cyber security.
- Funding: seed pilots (profitable, measurable), blended finance to scale, reserve liquidity for shocks.
- Op-sec: compartmentalise sensitive plans; strict data minimisation; secure comms and identity hygiene.
- Influence: coalition-building, discreet philanthropy, evidence-backed research, local partners, media literacy.
- Scale: validate pilots, publish results, hand off to trusted partners for scaling and legitimacy.

Batman-Style 90-Day Operational Plan (starter)
Week 1–2: Mission & legal scaffolding
- Finalise mission statement and non-negotiables; create simple governance documents.
- Incorporate two legal vehicles: one operating LLC and one charitable/trust vehicle for grants.

Week 3–4: Intelligence & team
- Recruit 3 core advisors: lawyer (compliance), impact operator, operator with field experience.
- Build an OSINT watchlist and a simple dashboard (policy, supply-chain, competitor moves).

Week 5–8: Pilot selection & setup
- Choose 1 high-leverage pilot (e.g., modular clean-water system or regenerative microfarm).
- Run legal/due-diligence, secure permits, sign 1–2 local partner MOUs; budget seed capital.

Week 9–12: Proof, measurement, and safeguards
- Launch pilot, collect KPIs (impact, unit economics, user consent metrics), publish transparent summary.
- Harden op-sec, set emergency liquidity, and draft scale funding plan.
