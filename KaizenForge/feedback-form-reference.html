The Orbit concept

THE ONE-MINUTE PITCH

Orbit is an adaptive growth system for people pursuing more than one kind of life.

It turns routines into minimum, standard, and stretch versions; balances progress across domains like learning, health, creativity, money, faith, and relationships; adapts when plans change; and treats recovery from a slip as progress instead of resetting you to zero.
