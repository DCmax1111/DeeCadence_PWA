FIRST RUN GUIDE

How Orbit works

1. Pick a small proof

Do the full task when you can. Use the minimum version when life gets heavy.

2. Stack habits

Attach one action to another: after prayer, read one verse; after shower, open notes.

3. Log friction

Bad habits are not moral verdicts. Record resisted/slipped, the trigger, and the replacement.

4. Review gently

Orbit suggests what to protect, reduce, or move based on real history.
