Welcome to Dee_Cistem
Let's get you started with your growth journey. Here's how to use the app:
1
Explore Modules: Tap any colored module card on the home screen to open it. Each module tracks a different aspect of your growth.
2
Log Activities: Use the log buttons in each module to record your progress. Your stats update automatically and affect your overall rank.
3
Track Progress: Check the dashboard for an overview of all your stats. Ranks go from E to SS — consistency matters more than perfection.
4
Customize: Use the theme toggle for light/dark mode, and the username button to show your full name or initial.
5
Get Help: Tap the Feedback button anytime to share ideas or report issues. Your input shapes the app!
