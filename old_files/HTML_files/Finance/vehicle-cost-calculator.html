What the prototype prevents

The calculator separates statutory charge assumptions from owner-entered cost assumptions. If policy data is stale, it hides totals instead of letting an old rate look official.

03 Input guardrails

Negative numbers, missing prices, and stale tax assumptions are prevented before totals are displayed.
